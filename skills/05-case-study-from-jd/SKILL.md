# Tailor the case study to a JD

## Read first
- `case-study/README.md` for the headline numbers and how each was measured.
- `case-study/data/*.csv` when you need to check or recompute a number.

## Steps
1. Read the JD. Pick the 2–3 themes it rewards most:
   - 0→1 or ownership → the problem (no ATS) and why the tool was built in-house rather than bought.
   - AI or automation → the operating model and the guardrails.
   - Data or metrics → the funnel, the four-dimension measurement, and the finding that the bottleneck moved to interviewer availability.
   - GTM or scale → target users, zero-migration positioning, the roll-out estimate.
   - Talent or recruiting → shortlist rate, round-1 pass-through, hires.
2. Write the requested output (bullets, a 2-minute story, or a page) using only numbers from `case-study/`.
3. Keep measured and estimated numbers distinct. Effort minutes are estimates; cycle times, funnel counts and reliability counts are measured.
4. Offer likely follow-up questions with short answers, including the honest limits: small sample, and the quality trend started before the agent went live.

## Rules
- Do not invent numbers or outcomes.
- Keep candidates anonymous (IDs only).
- Change emphasis and wording, never facts.
